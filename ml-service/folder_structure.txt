Notes:
- Generated __pycache__ files, local caches, the virtual environment, and .env are excluded from this snapshot.
- Runtime data, model checkpoints, and generated artifacts are represented by their stable directory layout rather than every generated file.
- The registry is nested under settings and diseases; fusion unwraps diseases before iteration.
- Biomarker validation predictions use the long schema: dataset, target, true, prediction.
- ECG validation and biomarker validation artifacts are written under artifacts/metrics/.
- EchoNet video processing is OpenCV-based; PyAV is intentionally not a dependency.
